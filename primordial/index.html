Primordial

Six founding lineages share a shallow sea. Each creature steers with a small neural network it inherited, eats what its diet allows, and buds or mates when it is well fed. Copies are imperfect, so lineages drift apart, and when a lineage drifts far enough from its founder it is named as a new species.

Five of the founders graze algae. One scavenges carcasses and may learn to hunt. What happens next is up to selection.

Click a creature to see what it sees and how it thinks. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom. The panel on the right tracks every species that has ever lived.
